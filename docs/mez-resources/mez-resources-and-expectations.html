The MEZ Community​

  • Everything the MEZ does is a tradeoff in terms of what to use our resources on
  • More technical mentors vs more materials for teams vs robot transportation etc
  • We want teams to be aware of what we can offer
  • We also request teams respect our guidelines and requests
  • If you have suggestions for improving things, please speak up
  • I promise I listen to and consider every suggestion - even if I don’t end up doing it

Resources - Programs​

  • Robotics
  • Fall workshops
  • Detroit City Championship
  • Unified Robotics
  • MEZ build season
  • Student summer camp
  • Teacher/mentor workshops
  • Other programs
  • Career and College Advising
  • Future Ready Scholars
  • One-off opportunities such as career days, etc

Resources - Robotics​

  • Food every day
  • Access to technical mentors and machine shop supervision
  • Parts and raw materials
  • Access to tools and machines
  • Access to a serviceable practice field and sufficient game pieces
  • Robot transportation to and from almost all events
  • Technical assistance at events, within resources
  • Community with a large number of other schools and teams
  • Partnership with other Detroit organizations such as DHDC
  • Bus support for DPSCD teams
  • A table for your team where you can leave your stuff
  • Probably more…

Expectations​

As part of the community, we also have some things we request from you

Expectations - Everyone​

  • Treat everyone with respect
  • Cooperate with MEZ staff when they have a request
  • Help clean the facility when asked
  • Clean up after yourselves every day
  • In food areas (lunch/dinner, and the break room)
  • In the machine shop or common work areas
  • On the floor where you’re working (shavings, parts, debris, etc)
  • Move tables as needed
  • Follow all safety rules
  • Safety glasses
  • Ears unobstructed
  • Loose hair/clothing/accessories/etc tied back
  • Please, no singing/whistling/clapping/loud music/etc

Expectations - Coaches​

  • Serve as the supervisor and responsible adult for all your team members
  • This means enforcing the expectations as needed
  • The MEZ cannot supervise 100 students - we are relying on you
  • Legal responsibility for your team members
  • Students should not be at the MEZ before you arrive or after you leave
  • Please ensure they are gone before departing each day
  • Serve as the leader of your team
  • Take responsibility for your build - MEZ mentors are here to assist, but not to lead your team
  • Come with a plan each day that doesn’t wholly rely on MEZ staff
  • Work to engage your students and minimize the number in the break room
  • Make an effort to submit bus requests by the deadline and minimize the number of cancellations/last-minute changes
