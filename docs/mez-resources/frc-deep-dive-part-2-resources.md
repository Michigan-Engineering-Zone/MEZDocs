# FRC Deep Dive Part 2 - Resources

## FRC Deep Dive Part 2 Resources

MEZ Teacher/Coach Workshop

## Kit of Parts

- These are the parts you get on kickoff day each year
- You always get a season-specific box, and could get more than that (next slide)
- Likely everyone will get some controls stuff next year because of SystemCore
- You’ll have to go into the dashboard at some point, generally September or October, and select the MEZ as your kickoff pickup site. Keep an eye out for FIRST Team Blasts that talk about the deadline.
- Always inventory your kit of parts when you get it!

## Kit of Parts - Options

- You can opt out of the kit of parts chassis and the black tote
- If you’re doing swerve, opt out of the chassis to get a $450 AndyMark voucher
- The black tote contains what you need to build a KitBot, so if you want to do a KB or EveryBot, definitely keep that
- Otherwise, you can opt out to get a ~$200 voucher for Rev
- You can also pre-order additional game pieces, but at the MEZ there’s generally no need to do this
- Teams building elsewhere may want to consider it

## Kit of Parts - Virtual

- There is a “virtual” kit of parts that you can access in your FIRST dashboard
- This contains licenses for various software products
- It also contains vouchers for discounted or even free stuff
- Often you have until mid or late May to use some of the vouchers
- It’s worth scrolling through and seeing if anything interests you!

## Team Avatars

- You can make a 40x40 pixel avatar to represent your team at the events
- If you don’t, you get a default FIRST logo
- You can only upload this twice per season because they’re manually reviewed by a human
- Having an avatar adds branding and character to your team for everyone to see
- If you have an artist on your team, they can make one, but also now with AI it’s easy to create something better than the default

## Regranting

- If you get grants through FIRST, you can have FIRST regrant them to any nonprofit org
- This is done through the FIRST dashboard
- If you use 313 as a fiduciary, you can have them send the money there
- Just make sure to do this before the end of the school year

## FIRST Choice

- A program that lets you get free stuff that was donated to FIRST by vendors
- There’s a lot of junk, but some good stuff too
- You get credits each year and bid on items with a “priority list”
- There are typically two rounds
- It’s worth the time to browse and make a list each year

## FIRST Grants Page

- FIRST has a [page with grant opportunities](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/team-grant-opportunities)
- Many require mentors or parents with a connection to an org, but not all
- It’s work checking every once in a while
- Just apply! You never know what will happen.

## FIRST Scholarships

- Many colleges offer FIRST-specific scholarships
- FIRST has a [website page](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/scholarships) dedicated to this, with a searchable list
- This is up to your students to act on, but good to be aware of
- Reminder that the MEZ offers Career and College Advising as well
