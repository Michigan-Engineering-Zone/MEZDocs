# Bus Requests (DPSCD Teams)

DPSCD Teams: please use these forms to request your buses each week. Note: these forms are only for DPSCD teams that are confirmed as working at the MEZ. If you are not a MEZ team, or not a DPSCD team, these forms are not what you're looking for.

During the fall, bus requests will be due by 9 am on Fridays. During build, they will be due by 9 am on Wednesdays. Forms open automatically about two weeks in advance, and close automatically when they're due. Please let us know immediately if you have to cancel a trip, and please read the full bus details below. Thank you!

**Bus Request Guidelines** – Please read thoroughly.

- The bus system is administered by Phoebe and Cameron. Please include both of us on any emails. Please do not send bus-related inquiries to other MEZ staff members.
- The buses are fulfilled by ABC Student Transportation. The MEZ does not control the buses ourselves; we aggregate your requests and submit orders. If you have an urgent issue such as a bus not showing up, your first point of contact should be Phoebe who will re-confirm that the bus is scheduled. Your second point of contact is ABC dispatch, and your third point of contact is Cameron.
- Please submit bus request forms before 9 am Wednesday during build, or 9 am Friday in the fall, the week before the the bus trips.
- The forms open two weeks in advance of the deadline. This is to prevent scheduling trips several weeks or months in advance and then having to cancel. **Note that every cancellation costs several hundred dollars**
- We may send a link to the form in a reminder email but please do not count on that; please get in the habit of using the website.
- Phoebe or Cameron will send a confirmation email with a readout of buses for the week. **If you don't get a confirmation email or do not see your bus on the list, you do not have a bus, so *please* check the confirmations.** If the email seems wrong, please reach out immediately; with the volume of buses we handle, mistakes do happen, which is why we send the confirmations.
- Buses typically show up at your school around 4:30 on weekdays and 10:45 on Saturdays.
- If a bus is not present within 7-10 minutes after the scheduled pickup time, please either call your regular bus driver, or Phoebe, immediately. If you wait 30+ minutes, it will be too late to resolve the situation and still get your team to the MEZ that night.
- We request cancellations be submitted at least 48 hours in advance. Obviously, more is better. Any less and DPSCD will be charged for the bus regardless. **Please work with your team to ensure that your team is ready and able to attend the MEZ and ride the bus on any given day, *before* you schedule the bus.**
- If you need to change or cancel a bus, please email both Phoebe and Cameron. We need it in writing; there is so much going on during meetings that mentioning it verbally is not a reliable method.
- Late additions can sometimes be accommodated so you can ask, but there are no guarantees since this goes through a third party. Please try to avoid this if possible. If you do need to do this, the sooner you can send the request, the more likely they will fulfill it.
- Buses for competitions/field trips/etc will generally be handled separately from the standard form.
- If a day (such as a competition or holiday) has a special form sent for it but also happens to appear on a weekly form, only the response on the special form will be used. We try to avoid this scenario, but it does sometimes happen.

If you have any questions, please email them to Cameron and Phoebe.
