## Fundraising Goals

- Why are you raising money? This dictates how much money you need
- A DPSCD MEZ team could probably operate indefinitely without fundraising
- But, if a team wants to be very competitive, it can get expensive quickly

## Options

- There are four main ways teams raise money
- Grants
- Sponsorships
- Fundraising
- Team dues
- Different situations favor different methods
- Grants and sponsorships tend to be higher $/hour overall, but are high variance

## Sponsorships

- Companies strongly prefer sponsoring organizations they have a connection to
- For corporations, that typically means if an employee is affiliated with the team
- Reach out to all of your parents
- If they work for an automaker, that’s fantastic (GM is the best)
- Even if not, their company may be willing to give $500
- Companies that focus on community are good options
- Credit unions, car dealerships, etc
- You can go door to door with your students and solicit companies
