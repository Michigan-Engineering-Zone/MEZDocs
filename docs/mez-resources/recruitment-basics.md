## Recruitment at a Glance

Keep it simple!!!

- Identify your needs & set goals
- Lean on your community
- Expand & scale when you’re ready

## First Steps

Identify your needs

- Students? Mentors?
- Areas of Interest (Mechanical, Programming, Design, Business, etc.)

Set goals

- Students
- Entry-level goal: 2-3 students
- Mentors
- Entry-level goal: 1 mentor

## Next Steps

Lean on your community

- School
- Every event is an opportunity (Back to School nights, Level Ups, sporting events, STEM nights, assemblies)
- Staff -> potential mentors - not just teachers! Academic Interventionists, Clerical Staff, etc.
- Work with staff to recruit students
- Work with students to recruit students
- Family & Friends - Introduce them to FIRST
- Share updates! In person, social media, etc.
- Invite them to events
- Show them it’s accessible to support (once a week, etc.)

## Expand & Scale

- Social media / website
- Supporting FLL & FTC
- Entry-level: One school, One day
- Can be helpful to recruit students and sometimes even mentors!
- Creating new opportunities for recruitment & awareness
- Speaking at PDs
- Admin sending emails
