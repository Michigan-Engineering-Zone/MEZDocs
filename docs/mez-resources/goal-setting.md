# Goal-setting

## 2027 Values and Goal-setting

MEZ Teacher/Coach Workshop

[tinyurl.com/mezgoals](https://tinyurl.com/mezgoals)

## Different Objectives

- Every team has different motivations and values
- Being competitive on the field
- Building something cool
- Winning certain awards
- Reaching student development milestones
- What is meaningful to your team?

## Defining Goals

- A wide range of goals are all good
- What matters more than your goals is that your team has similar goals, or at least some shared goals
- Sometimes goals conflict with each other
- As coaches we have a lot of influence on our students and their goals
- Of course, that influence has limits too
- Having everyone understand the team goals is important

## Goals Activity

- Discuss the handout worksheet in a small group (3-4 people, at least two schools)
- You don’t have to fill it out if you don’t want to, but at least consider it
- After a few minutes we’ll share out to the larger group
